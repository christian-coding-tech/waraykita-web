Frequently Asked Questions

How do I search for products?

Go to the Explore tab in the sidebar. You can use the search box to search by name or description, filter by color or stock status, and sort results by newest, oldest, price, or name.

How do I save products to my favorites?

Click the heart icon on any product card. Your favorites are saved automatically in your browser and appear in the Favorites tab.

How do I change my profile picture?

Open the Profile tab, click on your avatar or "Click to change photo", select an image, then press Save Changes.

How do I customize the theme of the dashboard?

Open the Settings tab. You can change the primary, sidebar, success, and danger colors, or apply one of the quick theme presets. Your preferences are saved automatically and apply across the whole system.

How do I change my password?

Go to the Profile tab and use the "Change Password" form. Enter your current password, choose a new one (at least 8 characters), and confirm it.

Where can I find the system requirements?

Waray Kita works best in modern browsers like Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari. An internet connection is required to load products and use real-time features.
